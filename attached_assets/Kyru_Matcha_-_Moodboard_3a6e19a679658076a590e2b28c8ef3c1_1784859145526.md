# Kyru Matcha - Moodboard

**IMPORTANT: This file will be your website design instruction and prompt for Hardware Coffee Co.  Please read and learn it carefully before generating the website!**

Instagram: https://www.instagram.com/kyrumatcha/

| Category | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Color Palettes | **Base `#F1EFE8` warm bone white**, inspired by uncoated paper, gallery walls, and Kyru’s off-white campaign graphics. This should cover roughly 65–70% of the website. **Primary `#181916` softened ink black** for headlines, navigation, borders, and functional text. **Brand Accent `#264866` faded denim blue**, taken from Kyru’s calendar posts, tea packaging, and printed event materials. It should feel slightly washed and textural rather than digitally saturated. **Product Accent `#76805B` dry matcha green** for subtle availability markers, hover states, drink details, and occasional image-led sections. **Support `#B8B7B0` brushed stainless grey** referencing preparation trays, tins, counters, and utilitarian café equipment. **Secondary `#796957` roasted brown** for hojicha, shadows, packaging, and warmer photographic moments. A sharper **cobalt `#3049C9`** may appear only in handwritten annotations or one-off campaign graphics. Recommended balance: **70% bone white, 20% black, 5% denim blue, 3% matcha green, 2% supporting neutrals.** Avoid sage-heavy branding, gradients, bright greens, excessive beige, and decorative color combinations. |
| Typography | Use **PP Neue Montreal, Neue Haas Grotesk, or Suisse International** as the primary typeface. For an accessible alternative, use **Instrument Sans**. Headlines should be bold but not loud, with weights between **500–600**, tight tracking around **0.04em**, and compact line-height between **0.9–1.0**. Hero typography can be oversized, but it should remain restrained and flush to the grid rather than playful. Body copy, navigation, and buttons should use the same grotesk family at **12–16px**, mostly regular weight, creating a consistent editorial system. Use **IBM Plex Mono or DM Mono** for dates, locations, batch names, event listings, product details, and availability. Mono text should be small, uppercase, and functional, resembling exhibition labels or packaging information. For rare campaign moments, use a high-contrast italic serif such as **Cormorant Garamond Italic or Instrument Serif Italic**, particularly for words like “giveaway,” collaboration names, or limited releases. Handwritten notes should remain scanned or manually drawn graphic assets rather than a permanent handwriting font. The overall typography should feel designed, understated, and slightly archival. |
| Overall Mood | **A quiet, design-led matcha project that feels discovered rather than advertised.** The website should combine the restraint of a contemporary furniture studio, the raw documentation of an independent pop-up, and the cultural awareness of a small editorial publication. It should feel minimal but not sterile, premium but not luxurious, and personal without becoming cute. Use large areas of negative space, thin grid lines, compact navigation, small functional text, restrained typography, and only a few carefully selected images. Photography should appear as evidence of real moments rather than promotional content: a single drink under hard sunlight, a matcha tin on stainless steel, an event poster, or people naturally occupying a space. Avoid large photo galleries, repetitive drink images, rounded cards, decorative illustrations, mascots, shadows, stickers, and obvious matcha clichés. Copy should be brief, lowercase, and slightly cryptic, allowing visitors who already know Kyru to feel included while giving new visitors just enough context. The visual tone should communicate **“serious about the product, casual about everything else.”** The final result should feel like an archive, event noticeboard, and product catalogue made by people with strong taste who do not need to explain themselves too much. 🙂 |
